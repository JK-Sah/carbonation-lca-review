# Inclusion criteria — narrowed, v2 (23 August 2026)

Supersedes the criteria in the submitted manuscript. Narrower and operational: each clause is
checkable against the paper's own text, and every exclusion cites the clause it failed.

## Include only if ALL of the following hold

| | Criterion | How it is checked |
|---|---|---|
| **I1** | Peer-reviewed primary study reporting an **original** life-cycle assessment | Not a review, editorial, or secondary synthesis of others' inventories |
| **I2** | A **declared functional unit** and a system boundary of at least cradle-to-gate | Stated in a methods or goal-and-scope section |
| **I3** | The object assessed is a **cement-based product** — concrete, mortar, block, precast unit, aggregate, or binder | Not a process, a plant, a region, or a building |
| **I4** | The product is made using **accelerated carbonation curing, CO2 injection during mixing, or CO2 mineralisation of a cementitious feedstock** | The intervention must be applied to the product, not merely discussed |
| **I5** | A **quantified GWP or CO2 outcome** is reported for that product **against a stated non-carbonated comparator** | Both arms must be reported, or a difference plus one arm |
| **I6** | The value is **locatable** — traceable to a specific number in text, table, or figure | Recorded as page/table/figure in the extraction record |

## Exclude, with coded reason

| Code | Reason |
|---|---|
| **X1** | Natural carbonation studied as durability or service-life only |
| **X2** | Geological CO2 storage with no cement-based product |
| **X3** | **Uptake-only materials study** — CO2 balance, TGA, or sequestration measurement without life-cycle accounting |
| **X4** | **Scale mismatch** — global, national, or sectoral potential rather than a product-level assessment |
| **X5** | Review, meta-analysis, or reanalysis of inventories published elsewhere |
| **X6** | **No non-carbonated comparator**, so no effect can be formed |
| **X7** | Not a cement-based product |
| **X8** | Full text not retrievable |

## Effect on the five studies already read

| Study | Verdict | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Monkman & MacDonald 2017 | **Include** | I1-I6 met. 4.6% vs reference control mix, 1 m3 |
| Huang et al. 2019 | **Include** | I1-I6 met. 292-454 vs 419 kg CO2-eq/m3, 1 m3 |
| Tanzer et al. 2021 | **Include** | I1-I6 met. Table 3 scenarios vs 280 kg CO2/m3 benchmark, 1 m3 |
| Skocek et al. 2020 | **Exclude — X3** | CO2 balance stated "disregarding any indirect emissions"; no functional unit; comparator is limestone substitution, not a non-carbonated product |
| Ravikumar et al. 2021 | **Exclude — X5** | A reanalysis of 99 datasets drawn from 19 other publications. Including it would double-count those sources, and its unit of analysis is the dataset, not a product |

**Ravikumar is excluded from the pooled set but is central to the discussion.** It is the most
rigorous cross-study evidence available on the question, and its 19 source publications are
themselves candidates for this review — they are being checked against I1-I6 individually. This
treatment is the standard handling of an overlapping synthesis and should be stated explicitly
in the Methods.

## Why this is stricter than the submitted version

The submitted criteria required only "quantitative GWP or CO2 reduction for a concrete/
cementitious product produced by accelerated carbonation curing". That admits uptake-only
materials studies (X3) and sector-scale assessments (X4), which are not commensurable with
product-level LCAs and were a principal source of the heterogeneity Reviewer 2 questioned at
comments 2.1 and 2.18. Excluding them by rule, rather than absorbing them into a pooled
estimate, is the direct answer to that criticism.

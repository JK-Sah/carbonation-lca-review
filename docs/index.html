How this works

ΔG = A + U·(1 − s/η) − P   ·   r = ΔG / G_base × 100   ·   A = (clinker displaced) × 0.87

A transparent first-order life-cycle model: the net avoided GWP is the sum of avoided clinker emissions (A) plus mineralized uptake discounted by its supply-chain cost, U·(1 − s/η), minus any strength-compensation penalty P. Carbonation curing is genuinely cleaner when ΔG > 0 — i.e. when the CO₂ supply-chain intensity s stays below the break-even s* = η·(1 + (A − P)/U).

This is a screening model for building intuition and comparing options — not a substitute for a full ISO 14040/14044 LCA or a verified EPD. Clinker emission factor fixed at 0.87 kg CO₂e/kg. Parameter ranges are illustrative and grounded in the reviewed studies.
